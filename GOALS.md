# CashNarra goals

- Branch created for this programme: `work/cashnarra-v1`

## Outcome

Complete the agreed CashNarra V1 while preserving the existing Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, and deterministic ledger architecture.

## V1 success

### Record

- Personal and business workspaces.
- Cash, Bank, and MoMo accounts.
- Known and unknown opening balances.
- Typed narration and voice narration.
- Several events in one narration.
- Material clarification, compact review, and atomic authoritative save.
- Corrections.
- Debts, payables, receivables, transfers, loans, and owner money.
- Operating expenses incurred on credit, settled later without a second expense.

### Understand

- Searchable records and traceable provenance.
- Daily, weekly, and monthly Money Stories.
- Basic reports and honest unknown states.
- No fabricated profit.
- Private exports.

### Plan

- Goals with deterministic current-pace calculations.
- Honest insufficient-data behaviour.
- Separate what-if scenarios with visible assumptions.

### Recovery

- Offline and local draft capture.
- Failed-save recovery and request identity.
- Logout and shared-device safeguards.

### Voice

- Real recording, transcription, and an editable transcript.
- Ghana-relevant representative quality evaluation.
- Text fallback always available.
- No automatic posting.

### Quality

- Narra v2 preserved.
- Mobile usability.
- Accessibility evidence without claiming certification from automation alone.
- Financial tests, tenant-isolation evidence, and build, type, lint, and test evidence.

## Hard constraints

No major rewrite. Preserve exact-decimal money, balanced journals, atomic commits, idempotent retries, correction history, and provenance. AI proposes and explains. Deterministic code posts and reports.

## Non-goals

CashNarra V1 is not a bank, wallet, MoMo payment initiator, card issuer, lender, investment adviser, tax filing system, accountant replacement, POS, full inventory system, payroll, HR, CRM, ERP, or automatic bank/MoMo aggregation platform.

Also out of scope unless a newer user instruction says otherwise: receipt OCR, statement import, rich collaboration, automatic transaction ingestion, and sophisticated forecasting.

## External launch gates

Code completion does not authorise production launch. Production Supabase, SMTP, commercial hosting, backup objectives, provider agreements, Ghana data-protection process, BoG/PFTSP applicability, GRA software/record rules, NITA applicability, trademark clearance, and real-customer launch approval stay outside autonomous coding. Do not purchase or enable production services without explicit user approval.
