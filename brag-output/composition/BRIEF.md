## Intent

CashNarra — Make Room for Tomorrow. A cinematic, intimate, grounded 55–60s vertical
film. One fictional Ghanaian business owner (Ama, of the kit's fictional "Ama Works")
at closing time: the day's money fragments crowd her mind, resolve into CashNarra's
actual records through a visible product interaction, then open into room to look
ahead. Busy mind → somewhere to put the story → reviewed facts → room to look ahead.
Campaign line: "Make room for tomorrow." Treat the owner as capable and hardworking.

The user's duration requirement (55–60s) overrides any shorter default.
