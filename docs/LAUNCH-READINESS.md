# Launch readiness — unresolved external decisions

This checklist records questions carried forward from the supplied research and stack transcripts. It is not a fresh legal opinion or a statement that every recommendation is a statutory requirement.

- Identify the operating company, accountable contact, privacy controller and support process before collecting real customer information.
- Confirm Ghana DPC registration and applicable data-protection duties with qualified local advice. Finalise lawful bases, notices, processor contracts, access/deletion handling and incident procedures.
- Document Supabase, OpenAI, email and hosting locations, subprocessors, cross-border safeguards, retention and backup deletion. `store:false` is not guaranteed zero retention.
- Resolve the source questions about BoG/PFTSP scope for this manual recordkeeping product. Do not imply payment, lending, investment or regulated-service approval; these capabilities are deferred.
- Resolve GRA software-approval applicability, required retention and Ghana-held tax-record requirements. Distinguish statutory record retention from optional narration retention. Define an operator-reviewed deletion process before fulfilling requests.
- Confirm applicable NITA and related cybersecurity obligations, distinguishing requirements from recommendations and unresolved interpretations in the source research.
- Complete name/trademark clearance separately from using the proposed Narra identity in this development build. Preserve font/icon/campaign licences; the fictional campaign model is not a customer.
- Review Vercel commercial eligibility and pricing before deployment. Do not assume Hobby permits commercial use. No deployment is authorised by the build itself.
- Test hosted Auth recovery/confirmation and Resend delivery, private cache headers, RLS/RPC isolation, recovery, backups and restore. Arrange a security review and keyboard/screen-reader/accessibility review.
- Before live AI, approve a monetary budget, provider settings and representative Ghanaian narration evaluation. Keep unsupported or ambiguous inputs in review. Voice remains disabled until recording/upload validation and quality checks pass.
- Establish a trusted scheduled source-minimisation job, operational request inbox and documented retention holds. Pending account-data requests currently require operator handling.

Receipt OCR, statement imports/reconciliation, automatic bank/MoMo/SMS feeds, payroll/ERP, collaboration, public links, payments, lending, investment advice and tax filing remain deferred.
