Concept B — The Story Log

Your money, told day by day. The person's words stay beside the records they produced, so provenance is the default view and the home screen becomes a history worth returning to. The composer is always one tap away. CashNarra never replies in prose; it asks one material question and shows rows.

A month of days becomes an answer.

Money Stories add the days up and show the records behind every figure. Net movement is not profit; CashNarra says so, and asks about costs before it works profit out.
